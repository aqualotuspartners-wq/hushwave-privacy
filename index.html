Privacy policy

White Noise Sleep: Hushwave
Aqua Lotus Partners
Effective November 24, 2026

Hushwave plays relaxing background sounds that are generated on your device. This policy explains what the app does with information. The app is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition, including insomnia, tinnitus, or colic.

Information the app collects

Aqua Lotus Partners does not collect, sell, or store personal information. Hushwave does not require an account. Mixes you save are kept in a local database on your phone. The app does not use the microphone, does not read your contacts or files, and does not request your location.

If Android backup is turned on for this phone, Android may include the app’s local data in your own device backup. That backup is handled by your device account, not sent to Aqua Lotus Partners.

Advertising and consent

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. Before an ad is requested, the app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present a consent choice where one is required.

AdMob may collect and use the Android Advertising ID and related device information to deliver and measure ads, under Google’s policies. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android settings, and you can limit ad personalization there. Where the consent form is shown, you can also change your choice.

Ads are not placed on the dim bedside screen. A full-screen ad is not shown while sound is playing, and it is not shown simply because you opened the app.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state, so the consent form and ads can load. Sound playback does not need a connection.
  • Advertising ID, used by AdMob.
  • Notifications and a media-playback foreground service, so sound can keep playing and you can pause or stop it from the notification.
  • Wake lock, so the phone can keep generating sound while the screen is off. The lock is released when playback pauses or stops.

Children

Hushwave is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this page with a new effective date.
